# Standalone content stack attribution

This file accompanies the locally composed Minosoft standalone content view.
The third-party archives remain unmodified and are fetched into the configured
out-of-source, hash-addressed artifact store.

- Faithful 32x is produced by the Faithful Resource Pack community and is used
  under the Faithful License version 3. Credit and current terms:
  <https://faithfulpack.net/license>.
- Vanilla Evolved 1.9.0 is created by Vixel and is listed by its official
  Modrinth release under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0:
  <https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/vanilla-evolved> and
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/>.
- Open Assets Lib version 1 is created by NightML (NightMareLore) and is listed
  by its official Modrinth release under CC BY 4.0:
  <https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/nightml-open-assets-lib> and
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.
- GUI Revision 2.1.0 is created by Vixel. Its archive-provided license and
  official project terms remain authoritative:
  <https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/gui-revision>.

The generated compatibility scaffolding and four procedurally rasterized
fallback textures are Minosoft-authored. Their image-generation design-reference
hashes are recorded in the generated pack's `provenance.json`; the reference
bitmaps are not required to reproduce the pack.
